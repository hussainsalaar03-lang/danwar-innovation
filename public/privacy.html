Privacy Policy & Data Protection

Last Updated: September 9, 2026 (9th September 2026 Special Edition)

🔒 1. Strict Zero-Data Retention Standard

Developed by Muhammad Iqbal Danwar from Khairpur Mir's, Sindh, Pakistan, Danwar AI operates on a strict zero-data retention model. We do not require registration, email sign-ups, or user passwords. Your prompts and private chats are never stored on any remote developer server.

💾 2. Local Device Storage

Your session history and conversations reside exclusively within your browser's local sandbox (localStorage). You hold total control to delete single chats or clear your browser cache to permanently eliminate your data.

⚡ 3. Ephemeral In-Memory Analysis

When uploading audio files, songs, images, documents, or code files for review, media buffers are ingested into transient memory solely to synthesize your answer, after which memory buffers are immediately purged.
